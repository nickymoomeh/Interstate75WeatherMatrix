How it works

The page calls Open-Meteo directly from your browser. Weather refreshes every 10 minutes; animation runs locally. Settings are remembered on this browser when storage is available. No Wi-Fi password or API key is required.

The 12-hour option deliberately omits AM/PM to preserve the real matrix layout. Three-digit Fahrenheit MIN/MAX values drop the decimal, matching the firmware. Use the Scene control to preview the daytime or night-time animations regardless of the real local time.

Rain preview cycles light → moderate → heavy → dry → original weather. Wet previews jump to their target water depth so you can see the effect immediately; dry conditions drain one row per minute. Live rain and snow build by one row every 30 seconds toward the current intensity’s target, rather than adding to a permanent reservoir. MIN/MAX show the whole day’s forecast.

Birds visit during daylight; stars and occasional shooting stars appear after sunset. UFOs visit both scenes. At night, the temperature is abducted on the hour and half-hour; the button works anytime. Festive divider lights, Santa with his reindeer and Christmas-coloured UFOs appear throughout December and January 1–14. Santa can visit by day or night. The preview buttons work at any time. The browser stays bright at night.
